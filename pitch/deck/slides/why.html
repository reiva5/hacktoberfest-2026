Why programs first? Four reasons. One: they have deadlines and eligibility rules, which is exactly where a model reading the page adds value over a listing. Two: it’s what founders look for first. Three: institutions already publish them, so the gateway is useful on day one without anyone signing up. Four: it needs no personal data, which matters for a public body.
