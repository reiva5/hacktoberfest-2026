Problem

Smallholders find out about crop disease too late

  • Blast and brown spot can wipe out 20–30% of a paddy harvest before farmers notice.
  • Extension officers each cover thousands of farmers, so advice arrives weeks late.
  • Flash floods are getting more frequent, and farmers have no plot-level warning.
