The problem

01

Which programs can I apply to?

Every ASEAN founder asks this. Directories list programs; they don’t tell a founder which ones they qualify for, or why.

Every founder in the region asks the same question: which of these programs can I actually apply to? Programs are scattered across government, corporate and VC sites, in five or more languages, and the eligibility rules are buried in long pages. Directories already list programs. None of them answer that question.
